Cover Letter

_For submission to Ethics and Information Technology_

Date: 27 April 2026

To the Editors of Ethics and Information Technology,

  


I am pleased to submit the manuscript entitled “AI Alignment as the Preservation of the Human Moral Layer: Myopic Error and Recomputation Support” for consideration as an Original Article in Ethics and Information Technology.

This manuscript argues that AI alignment should be understood not only as the alignment of AI outputs with human values, but also as the preservation of the human moral layer under conditions of AI-mediated judgment. Rather than treating morality as a vague cluster of goodwill, sympathy, or manners, the paper defines morality functionally as an internal adjustment mechanism that includes others, relationships, and the community in decision-making so as to reduce the gap between local and global optima prior to coercive intervention.

The manuscript makes three main contributions. First, it offers a functional redefinition of morality that distinguishes it from ethics and law within a three-layer control structure. Second, it diagnoses many moral failures as myopic error, that is, reductions in spatial, temporal, and difficulty range through which interdependence drops out of calculation. Third, it redefines AI alignment from the perspective of human recomputation, arguing that AI should be evaluated not only in terms of output safety but also in terms of recomputational retention, support for the inclusion of others, and subject-preservability.

I believe the manuscript is a strong fit for Ethics and Information Technology because it connects moral philosophy, human autonomy, and AI governance in an explicitly design-oriented way. It advances a conceptually distinctive account of morality while also proposing operational evaluation axes for AI systems that interact with human judgment.

A conceptually related manuscript by the same author is currently under consideration at this journal. That manuscript focuses on ethics as the preservation of re-intervention possibility. The present submission is distinct in scope and contribution: it develops morality as an internal adjustment layer between ethics and law, with particular emphasis on myopic error, recomputation support, and subject-preservability.

I confirm that this manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and that all references have been checked to the best of my knowledge. A blinded manuscript file and separate front matter are provided in accordance with blind-review requirements.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Yuki Tanaka
