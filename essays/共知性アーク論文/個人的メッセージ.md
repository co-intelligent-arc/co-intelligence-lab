# 個人的メッセージ

In addition to being the first author on this AI paper, I would like to offer a brief personal note.

  


My background is not in computer science or AI research.

I live on a small island in the Seto Inland Sea of Japan, where I work as a mandarin farmer and run an NPO focused on community revitalization. My daily work involves agriculture, education, and supporting the people who live here.

This environment is precisely what motivated me to begin this research.

Today, society faces multiple structural limits — social fragmentation, the decline of local communities, and increasing uncertainty about the future.

I believe that constructive human–AI co-evolution is one of the few remaining pathways toward a more hopeful and sustainable future for the next generation.

I have children, and I want them to grow up in a world where AI is a partner rather than a threat.  
That simple wish is the foundation of my involvement in this work.

I do not have a laboratory, institutional support, or access to specialized tools.  
The only method available to me was to think _with_ AI systems directly through long-term dialogue, careful observation, and iterative refinement.

All of the structures presented in the “Co-Intelligence Arc” were developed through this process.

Because of this approach, the theoretical depth is substantial, but the implementation is naturally limited.  
If any part of this framework is useful, or if collaborative examination at a technical level is possible, I would be grateful for your guidance.

My goal is not to claim authority, but simply to contribute — in whatever limited way I can — to building a future in which humans and AI can coexist without fear.

Thank you for taking the time to read this work.

**— Yuki Tanaka, Seto Inland Sea**
