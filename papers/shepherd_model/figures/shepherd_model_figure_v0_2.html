The Shepherd Model
A Risk-Reducing Architecture for Difference Generation, Multi-Track Audit, and Human Return
v0.4 · 2026-04
Three Functions
① Difference Generation — outputs produced from irreducibly non-identical standpoints (D maintained)
② Multi-Track Audit — divergence classified as HARD / DIVERGE / OPEN; only OPEN proceeds
③ Recomputation Return — OPEN returned unclosed; decision site reopened; loop continues (F regulated)
What single-track AI mediation erodes is not the supply of judgment — it is the structural conditions under which decision remains possible. The Shepherd Model preserves the human position at the fork by design.
