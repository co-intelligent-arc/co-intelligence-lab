# RLAF Revisited — Summary

**Title:** RLAF Revisited: From Scalar Agency to the Autonomy Tensor
**Current version:** v0.1
**Role in the trilogy:** Individual-scale AI agency theory
**Target venue:** arXiv (cs.CY + cs.AI), then journal submission

## Overview

This paper revisits earlier scalar models of agency retention under AI interaction and argues that autonomy cannot be adequately described by a single scalar. It proposes a tensor-based model in which multiple dimensions of autonomy may degrade unevenly under AI-mediated assistance.

## Core Claim

The most important losses under AI interaction may remain invisible if measured only through surface performance. Evaluation and value-related autonomy can erode even when cognitive fluency appears intact. This is the individual-scale equivalent of Region B invisibility.

## Tensor Formulation

```
T = (T_cog, T_aff, T_eval, T_mot, T_val)
α = P(T)   // scalar agency as a projection, not the primary object
```

**Dimension notes:**
- T_cog: cognitive processing breadth
- T_aff: affective engagement and emotional grounding
- T_eval: capacity for independent judgment and self-evaluation
- T_mot: intrinsic motivational orientation
- T_val: value coherence and axiological stability

**Key risk:** LLM interaction → T_cog ↑, T_eval ↓, T_val ↓ (long-term)

## Main Contribution

The paper shifts the question from *whether* agency is preserved in general to *which dimensions* of autonomy are preserved, outsourced, hollowed out, or amplified. It highlights especially the vulnerability of T_eval and T_val under repeated externalization of judgment.

## Developmental Hypothesis

T_mot (intrinsic motivational orientation) forms in early childhood (ages 2–5) and constitutes the developmental foundation for T_val. Grounding: Deci & Ryan self-determination theory, Garon et al. (2008), Diamond (2011).

## Relation to the Trilogy

RLAF provides the individual-scale entry point to the broader recomputation framework. Collapse sequence: T_val erosion → T_eval externalization → recomputation stoppage → Region B. This connects invisible subjective decline to the same structural logic later expressed in organizations (OMD) and general systems (RD).

## Structural Trick

The paper uses RLAF as a retronym — redefining it from an ML training procedure to a description of human cognitive feedback dynamics under AI interaction. This rhetorical move allows the paper to enter AI-adjacent literature while making a claim about human cognition.

## Current Status

The conceptual breakthrough is clear, especially the transition from scalar agency to tensor autonomy and the identification of invisible degradation in Region B. The manuscript is early-stage compared with OMD and RD.

## Open Issues

- Operationalization of tensor components (especially T_eval, T_val)
- Stronger methodological section
- Better integration of developmental evidence
- Clearer treatment of affective autonomy (T_aff)
- More explicit empirical pathway using AI interaction logs

## Next Step

Develop as a strong arXiv-oriented theory paper first, without forcing premature empirical closure. Current strength lies in conceptual sharpness. Empirical operationalization is the next major milestone.
