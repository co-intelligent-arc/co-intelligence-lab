## What this is

The individual-scale entry point of the trilogy. This paper argues that agency under AI interaction cannot be described by a scalar measure. It proposes a five-dimensional autonomy tensor in which dimensions degrade unevenly — and the most critical degradation remains invisible to surface performance metrics.

**Tensor formulation:**
```
T = (T_cog, T_aff, T_eval, T_mot, T_val)
α = P(T)   // scalar agency is a projection, not the primary object
```

**Key risk pattern:** prolonged LLM interaction → T_cog maintained, T_eval and T_val eroded. This is Region B at the individual scale: external functioning preserved while internal recomputation has stalled.

**Structural rhetorical move:** RLAF (Reinforcement Learning from AI Feedback) is redeployed as a retronym — reframed from an ML training procedure into a description of human cognitive feedback dynamics under AI interaction. This allows the paper to enter AI-adjacent literature while making a claim about human cognition.

**Connection to trilogy:** RLAF supplies the micro-foundation for u (update capacity) in the general theory (RD). Collapse sequence: T_val erosion → T_eval externalization → recomputation stoppage → Region B.

## Core claim

> The most important losses under AI interaction may remain invisible if measured only through surface performance. Evaluation and value-related autonomy can erode even when cognitive fluency appears intact — the individual-scale equivalent of Region B invisibility.
