## Overview

This series develops a unified theoretical account of **subjectivity, subject-injury, and subject protection** in the age of AI.

The central claim: subject-injury does not begin with visible breakdown. It proceeds as the **silent cessation of recomputation** — a condition in which surface functioning is preserved while internal updating has ceased. This invisible degradation can be widespread, advanced, and systematically undetected within high-performing institutional environments.

The series addresses three interconnected questions:

- **Conceptual:** What is a subject? Why does the individual-completeness model fail?
- **Dynamic:** How does subject-injury proceed? What is the minimal causal chain?
- **Design:** How should subjectivity be protected? What are the minimum institutional conditions?

## Core Concepts

### Subject (主体)

> A subject is an entity capable of updating the coherence between itself and its relational context through recomputation, while sustaining constraints across multiple hierarchical levels.

### Subject-Injury (主体侵害)

> Subject-injury is not destruction but invisible degradation: difference disappears, recomputation ceases, evaluative autonomy is eroded, and value autonomy becomes fixed — while output continues and the system remains socially legible as normal or successful.

### Region B (見かけ正常・内部停止)

> The state in which structural stability (σ) and cognitive autonomy (T_cog) remain while recomputation capacity (M) and evaluative autonomy (T_eval) have ceased and value autonomy (T_val) has become fixed. Standard performance indicators are structurally unable to detect this state.

### Difference-Retention (差異保持 / D)

> The preservation and availability of difference inputs capable of prompting re-evaluation of the subject's existing frame. The common upstream condition for recomputation across all three integrated frameworks (OMD, RD, RLAF).

```
ω↑ + L_c↑  →  C↑  →  D↓

  →  E_r_eff↓  →  M↓ / u↓  →  A(e,u) multiplicative degradation
  →  T_eval paralysis  →  T_val becomes fixed
```
